# Financial Literacy and Saving Behaviour among Students

## 1. Project Overview

Financial literacy plays an important role in how individuals manage money, make spending decisions, develop saving habits, and use digital payment methods. Understanding students' financial behaviour can help identify patterns in their financial awareness and money-management practices.

This project uses survey-based data to explore financial literacy, saving behaviour, spending patterns, and digital payment behaviour among students. The findings are presented through a Power BI dashboard, with dashboard screenshots included in the accompanying project report.

The project combines statistical analysis and data visualization to examine patterns in students' financial behaviour and communicate findings in an accessible format.

## 2. Research Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- To assess financial literacy among students.
- To explore students' saving habits and financial planning behaviour.
- To examine students' spending patterns.
- To understand the use of digital payment methods among students.
- To investigate relationships between financial literacy and financial behaviour.
- To summarize relevant findings through data visualization.

## 3. Research Questions

The project is guided by the following research questions:

1. What patterns can be observed in students' financial literacy?
2. How do students approach saving and managing their money?
3. What spending patterns are observed among students?
4. How do students use digital payment methods?
5. What relationships exist between financial literacy and saving or spending behaviour?
6. Which patterns in the survey data may help explain differences in students' financial behaviour?

The answers to these questions should be interpreted using the actual survey results and analyses.
